How posting a gig works

There is no self-service dashboard yet. For now, we do the matching by hand.

1

Sign up

Fill in the form with who you are and the kind of help you need.

2

We get in touch

We contact you to confirm the details: the work, the date, the place and the pay.

3

We look for students

We share your gig with students on our list who fit, and let you know who is available.

What we ask of you

Fair, clear and safe

  • Fair pay, paid on time. Agree the amount up front and pay students when you said you would.
  • A clear description. Say what the work is, where it is, how long it takes and what students should bring or wear.
  • Safe working conditions. Students should never be put at risk, and should have someone to report to on the day.
  • Respect for study time. Stick to the agreed hours. Students have lectures and tests to get to.
What we cannot promise yet

Where the pilot stands

  • We may not fill every gig. Our student list is still small, so we will tell you honestly if we cannot find the right people.
  • Short notice is hard. The more warning you give us, the better our chances.
  • We are still working things out. How we match, confirm and follow up may change as we learn.

What the pilot costs

Taking part in the pilot is free. You do not pay Varsity Gigs anything. You do pay the students we provide for the work they do, at the rate agreed before the gig starts.

The problem

Student work is scattered and hard to trust

Work lives in group chats

Student work in South Africa is mostly found through WhatsApp groups, word of mouth and social media posts. It is scattered, unreliable and often hard to trust.

Students lose out

Good gigs get buried in group chats, and there is no way to prove you are reliable.

Organisers lose out

Last-minute no-shows are common, and finding people you can trust takes time.

What we do

One place for reliable student gigs

Clearer expectations, a record of reliability, and less chasing for everyone.

For students

One profile in place of many group chats. Get vetted once, get considered for gigs, and build a rating and work history after each job.

For organisers and agencies

A vetted, rated standby pool, so you can fill shifts with people who have been checked and have shown up before.

How it works

Three steps

1

Get vetted.

Students join the standby pool and are checked before they are offered gigs.

2

Get booked.

Organisers request staff. Vetted students who fit are offered the work.

3

Get rated.

After each gig, both sides give feedback, and the student's track record grows.

Where we are now

We are early, and we will say so

Varsity Gigs started with peer tutoring and freelance work and is now focused on event and promo staffing. We are validating demand with students and organisers and running small pilots before building the full platform.

The vetted standby pool is being assembled now. We are not a large, established network yet.

Pilot results coming soon

We will share what we learn from our pilots here once we have permission to publish it.

Our promises

What we stand by

  • Free for students. Students never pay to join or to be considered for work.
  • Vetted, not random. People are checked before being offered gigs.
  • Rated both ways. Reliability is recorded and rewarded.
  • Privacy first. Student details are stored only with their consent and handled in line with POPIA.
  • Built for South Africa. Designed around how students here actually find work, mainly WhatsApp, and around real campus life.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is it free for students?

Yes. Students never pay to join or to be considered for work.

How does vetting work?

You fill in the intake form to join the standby pool, and a vetting step follows. Students are checked before they are offered any gigs.

How do organisers book?

Organisers request staff, and vetted students who fit are offered the work. See For organisers for how this runs during the pilot.

Is my data safe?

Your details are stored only with your consent and handled in line with POPIA. We use them to match you with gigs.

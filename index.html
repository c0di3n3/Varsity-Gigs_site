About Varsity Gigs

Gigs, but make it reliable.

Varsity Gigs connects South African students with real, paid gigs: event and promo staffing, tutoring and freelance work. Instead of chasing work in scattered WhatsApp groups, students build one profile, get vetted, and get booked. Organisers get reliable, rated people they can call on, including at short notice. Every gig builds a track record students keep.

Straight talk

What to expect right now

We want you to know exactly where things stand before you sign up.

  • Gigs are limited. We are starting small, so it may take a while before something suitable comes up for you.
  • No guarantee of work. Signing up puts you on our list. It is not a job offer.
  • Things will change. We are still learning what works, and how the platform runs may shift as we go.
  • Your feedback counts. Tell us what is working and what is not. That is the point of a pilot.
For organisers

Need a few reliable students?

If you run events, a small business or a campus society and need short-term help, we would like to hear from you. During the pilot we work with a small number of organisers and help with matching ourselves.

How it works for organisers

What we are looking for

Short, clearly described gigs with fair pay and safe working conditions. If that sounds like what you offer, you are who we want to pilot with.
